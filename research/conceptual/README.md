# Conceptual and historical research

This space is for carefully bounded conceptual, historical, and cross-disciplinary research that may help frame Fitrah questions. It is not a new layer of the accepted Lab 0 model and does not establish equivalence between machine systems and human, religious, legal, or historical concepts.

## Proposals under review

- [Issue #20](https://github.com/c0ldskin/fitrah/issues/20): proposed exploration of Amanah, Waqf, and continuing obligations in agent lifecycles.
- [Issue #21](https://github.com/c0ldskin/fitrah/issues/21): proposed prior-art review of algorithms or computational models relevant to theological and historical trust analogies.

These issues are open proposals. This directory adds no conclusion or accepted terminology.

## Scope and evidence rules

- Treat analogy as a source of questions, not proof that a software property exists or is novel.
- Distinguish primary texts and historical evidence from modern interpretation, engineering analogy, and the researcher's own hypothesis.
- Cite sources with enough detail to find and assess them; record translation, edition, date, and interpretive uncertainty when relevant.
- State where an analogy breaks down. Do not infer morality, conscience, faith, intent, or theological fitrah from machine behavior or software state.
- Compare computational models against established mechanisms and recent work before proposing implementation; use primary literature or official specifications for technical claims.
- Keep accepted invariants, assumptions, threat model, results, experiment code, and formal Note snapshots unchanged unless a separate TC/research review explicitly adopts a change.

Add topic notes here only when there is a clear research question, provenance, and review path. Link them from the register rather than treating this directory as a second source of truth.
